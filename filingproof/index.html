01 / DOCUMENTS

No automatic uploads

Parsing runs client-side. Document text is not included in exported reports; filenames and diagnostic metadata are. The parsing library itself is fetched over the network.

02 / LIMITATIONS

Signals, not certification

Searchable text is not proof of readable OCR. Privacy-pattern checks are incomplete. Signatures, redactions, encryption permissions, and legal filing eligibility require independent review.

03 / SOURCES

Rules are traceable

Built-in reference rules link to published filing guidance. This alpha does not automatically refresh court policies or determine a jurisdiction's current upload limits.

PRODUCT SCOPE / V0.1

What FilingProof actually does

PDF.js parses selected files in browser memory. The app evaluates a deterministic rules engine and creates a report of checks, warnings, items not evaluated, and source links.

Reference profiles are curated, versioned subsets of published technical guidance. They do not represent a complete court policy and may become outdated. The currently selected PDF is never transmitted to GreenKappa Labs servers.

Not supported: e-filing submission, virus scanning, complete active-content inspection, signature verification, redaction verification, substantive legal analysis, legal advice, privileged document storage, OCR, or guaranteed court acceptance.

If a document is sealed, restricted, or otherwise subject to organizational handling rules, obtain authorization before opening it in any third-party software.
