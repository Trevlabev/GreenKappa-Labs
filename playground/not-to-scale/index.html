How to play

  1. Each turn gives you 2 actions. Move to an adjacent tile using STEP.
  2. At a room containing an unclaimed Record, use TRACE (1 action). Find all three.
  3. Use SIGNAL (1 action) to protect your current room from the coming forecasted shift.
  4. Click End turn. Every third turn the forecast rearranges two rooms; your pawn travels with its room.
  5. Return to the Foyer and EXIT (1 action) with all three records before Shift 12.

About the prototype

This web demo simplifies the tabletop game: the entire nine-room floor plan starts visible, and adjacent tiles always connect. The physical prototype uses room placement, matching doors, rotation, Fold moves, Echo, Focus, tools, and other systems not simulated here.

There is no AI, account, tracking, or server-side game state. Refreshing resets progress.
