Not to Scale: The Shifting House

Explore nine rooms, recover three records, and get back to the Foyer before the twelfth Shift. Every third turn, two rooms swap places. If you're standing in one, you move with it.

This is a small browser experiment, not the full tabletop game. It leaves out door matching, room rotation, and several other physical rules.
