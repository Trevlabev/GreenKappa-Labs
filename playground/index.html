Not to Scale: The Shifting House

Explore nine impossible rooms, collect three records, and get everyone home before the house completes twelve shifts. Rooms move beneath your feet; every third turn, the forecast becomes real.

This browser microgame deliberately simplifies the physical card game's v0.3 rules. It is not a digital port or an approved rules substitute.
