01 / DEPENDENCY INTELLIGENCEWORKING ALPHA · v0.1

Flowglass

See what a change could affect before you make it.

Build and inspect a dependency map. Follow upstream or downstream relationships, find cycles or missing documentation, and export a readable impact review.

  • Editable graph and spatial layout
  • Multi-hop impact paths and cycle detection
  • JSON projects and Markdown reports
Launch Flowglass ↗
Scope and limitations

Graphs are supplied or modeled manually. No Power Automate or SharePoint tenant scanning, automatic discovery, cloud sync or production-risk certification. Data stays in the current browser unless exported.

02 / DOCUMENT DIAGNOSTICSWORKING ALPHA · v0.1

FilingProof

Find technical PDF problems before filing.

Examine PDF text layers, form fields, embedded attachments and selected reference checks with explicit not-evaluated findings.

  • Browser-side PDF review
  • Source-aware reference profiles
  • JSON and printable findings
Launch FilingProof ↗
Scope and limitations

Not a court filing service or acceptance validator. No legal advice, OCR, complete redaction checks, malware scanning or certification. Files are reviewed locally; a pinned PDF parsing library is fetched from a third-party CDN.

03 / OPERATIONS SIMULATIONWORKING ALPHA · v0.1

CourtFlow

Follow a fictional case from intake to closure.

Explore case queues, task dependencies, exceptions, assignments and a locally stored event trail using synthetic workflows.

  • Fictional intake and case queues
  • Dependency-checked tasks
  • JSON, Markdown and CSV exports
Launch CourtFlow ↗
Scope and limitations

Fictional data, fictional deadlines and synthetic business rules only. Not connected to courts, e-filing, identity systems or protected records. No authoritative legal workflow guidance or tamper-evident audit ledger.

04 / OPERATIONAL KNOWLEDGEWORKING ALPHA · v0.1

Handoff Atlas

Turn a complicated procedure into a navigable path.

Author branching runbooks, validate routes, publish local revision snapshots and guide an operator through decisions with portable handoff records.

  • Procedure authoring and route checks
  • Guided runs with version snapshots
  • Workspace and handoff exports
Launch Handoff Atlas ↗
Scope and limitations

Browser-local demonstration with sample procedures. No team accounts, encrypted backend, verified operator identity or signed audit log. Do not enter sensitive operational or case records.

Flowglass promotional artwork is illustrative rather than a screenshot. CourtFlow's preview is a schematic based on its existing workflow modules; the other visual panels are conceptual diagrams. Launch links open the actual applications.

02 / Design lab

Next, not yet.

Two proposed products with defined problems and validation gates. These are specifications and design explorations—there are no public applications to launch.

01 / EVIDENCE INTEGRITYCONCEPT · REQUIREMENTS STAGE

Proofline

Know what a claim rests on.

CLAIM / P-014↳ SOURCE / DOC-003↳ DECISION / REV-012TRACEABLE, NOT AUTOMATICALLY TRUE

A proposed evidence-linked document review workspace for policies, specifications and long-running project records. It would make supporting passages, decisions, revisions and unresolved contradictions inspectable.

  • Claim-to-passage traceability
  • Decision and change records
  • Stale-source and discrepancy review
Planned workflow and next gate

Register sources and decisions; link specific claims to evidence; compare new versions; export unresolved issues. Next: prototype a source-anchored issue register and evaluate false-positive flags with a human reviewer.

02 / NARRATIVE CONTINUITYCONCEPT · DATA MODEL STAGE

Lorelock

Let the world change without losing its history.

CHARACTERCANONEVENTRULES

A proposed continuity workspace for fiction, tabletop campaigns, sourcebooks and branching stories. It would keep author-approved canon distinct from in-world claims, drafts and spoilers while tracking relationships and timelines.

  • Canon and provenance states
  • People, places and relationship maps
  • Chronology and contradiction review
Planned workflow and next gate

Register accepted references and facts; capture scene outcomes and rule changes; compare new material against established continuity; record approvals. Next: test a continuity schema on one controlled fictional setting.

03 / Development principles

A useful interface
is only the start.

01 / TRACEABILITY

Show where it came from.

Make evidence, rules, assumptions and change history visible when decisions matter.

02 / HUMAN CONTROL

Keep judgment with the operator.

Provide reviewable routes and outputs rather than invisible automated authority.

03 / VALIDATION

Prove the critical path.

Separate a functioning prototype from field-tested, secure, supported production software.

Testing / Collaboration
