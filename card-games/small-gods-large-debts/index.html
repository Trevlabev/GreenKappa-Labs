01 / THE DIVINE ECONOMY

There is no such thing as a free miracle.

You can perform a Miracle with your own Devotion—or ask another god to sponsor it. Borrowing can help you gain Glory sooner, but the Covenant comes due at the end of your next turn. Every bargain creates a new strategic problem.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS / 21 NOV 2026

THE BILL
ISN'T FINAL.

Small Gods, Large Debts is a work in progress. Current testing explores eight divine identities, the Titan system, and a streamlined game without active Omens. The historical 54-card source inventory is preserved, but the revised physical print manifest, numerical balance, and actual human-play duration remain unsettled.
