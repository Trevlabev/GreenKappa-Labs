01 / The idea

The universe is full of gods. Unfortunately, most of them have very little power and exceptionally expensive ambitions.

In Small Gods, Large Debts, miracles are a currency, promises are leverage, and divine influence is rarely free. Build devotion and prestige while negotiating covenants that can reward you now and compromise you later. Every favor makes someone stronger. Every obligation gives someone else a claim.

02 / The player experience

01

Claim a little divinity

Take the role of a lesser god and work with the powers, resources, and ambitions available to you.

02

Bargain for belief

Grant miracles and trade favors to gain devotion and influence. The best deal is not always the cheapest one.

03

Live with the consequences

Negotiate covenants, account for divine debts, and respond to omens as rival gods turn promises into pressure.

What makes it differentPower is borrowed. The interest is eternal.

Every new promise gives someone leverage. Deciding who to owe can matter as much as the miracle you gain.
