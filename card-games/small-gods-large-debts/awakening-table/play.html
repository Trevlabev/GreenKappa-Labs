A game of divinity, debts, and terrible bargains

Small Gods, Large Debts

Choose your deity. Play a full match against two automated rival gods. Draw concealed Miracles, negotiate Covenant sponsorship, awaken three hidden Titans, and pursue Glory.

FIELD GUIDE · CANDIDATE RULES

How to win

Finish five rounds with the most Glory after debts are settled. Tied Glory? Most remaining Devotion wins; a complete tie is shared. A rare Titan effect can end the match early.

Devotion · spendable power

Start with 3; gain 3 at the beginning of each turn, up to 10. Pay for Miracles, Petitions and Titan awakenings. Saving it can protect you from debt.

Glory · victory score

Start at 0. Miracles and Petitions earn Glory. Defaults and some Titan effects can take it away. Devotion is not Glory.

Covenants · debt

A willing rival contributes 2 Devotion toward your Miracle; you owe them 3 on your next turn. Pay what you can; lose 1 Glory for each unpaid Devotion. Some abilities alter settlement.

Your turn, in order
  1. Worship: automatically gain 3 Devotion and draw one secret Miracle.
  2. Optional powers: use a deity ability when eligible; awaken one sealed Titan for 4 Devotion or invoke an awakened Titan once with the D8 (you may do both). Follow the card and power exceptions.
  3. Make one move: perform one Miracle from your hand, Petition for 4 Devotion to earn 1 Glory, or Gather 2 Devotion.
  4. End turn: resolve any due debt, retain no more than two Miracles, and pass to the next god. Some abilities can change when debt resolves.
What are Titans and Boons?

Three of eight Titans begin sealed. Awakening makes a Titan available for invocation; the eight-sided Die of Unmaking selects an effect. Natural rolls of 1 and 8 normally trigger Bad and Good Boons respectively; 2–7 resolve the Titan’s effect. The Unfinished Constant has a special conditional endgame effect at exactly 10 Devotion. Awakening is not required to score Glory.

A worked example: why a bargain costs more later

You have 2 Devotion and need 4 for a Miracle. A rival agrees to sponsor 2. Your own 2 plus their 2 pays the cost. The Miracle resolves and records a 3-Devotion debt. If you have only 1 Devotion available when forced to settle, you pay 1 and lose 2 Glory. The rival receives only the 1 you actually pay. This example assumes no modifiers or other effects.

Turn reference & debt
