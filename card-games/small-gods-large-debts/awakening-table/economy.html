01 / THE ECONOMY

Power is not victory.

Worship grants 3 Devotion per turn (maximum 10). Spend it on Miracles, a 4-Devotion Petition for 1 Glory, or Titan awakenings. Glory determines the winner; debt links today’s power to tomorrow’s risk.
