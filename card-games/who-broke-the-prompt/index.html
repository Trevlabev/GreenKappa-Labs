01 / The idea

A single instruction can change everything. Finding out who wrote it is the hard part.

Who Broke the Prompt? combines group problem-solving with hidden-role sabotage. Players collaboratively shape an AI prompt, then interpret the output as evidence. A surprising response may be an innocent misunderstanding—or proof that somebody has been quietly changing the mission.

02 / The player experience

01

Get the assignment

Your group receives a task to solve with a shared AI conversation.

02

Alter the prompt

Everyone contributes, but at least one participant is working toward a hidden objective rather than the team's success.

03

Interrogate the output

Inspect what the AI produces. Compare the result with the assignment and question the decisions that got you there.

04

Name the saboteur

Use the available evidence, table talk, and deduction to make an accusation.

What makes it differentThe AI is the witness. The players are the suspects.

The fun comes from social interpretation, not from pretending the model itself can identify the saboteur. Players create the evidence and argue over what it means.

03 / Art direction

An identity of its own.

Original high-resolution art from our game-design work. The cover concept is retained exactly as created; it has not been redrawn into substitute graphics.

The secondary image is a separate illustrated promotional sell sheet; its card samples are concepts, not approved print files. Artwork and packaging remain concept designs while the games are in development.
