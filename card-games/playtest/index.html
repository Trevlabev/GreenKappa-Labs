GreenKappa Games / Alpha 0.3

Who broke
the prompt?

A 4–8-player social-deduction game where everyone helps write an AI prompt, but one person secretly wants the assignment to fail. This is an unreleased alpha, not a retail product.

54 illustrated alpha cards / provisional

The brief is already in trouble.

Play three client commissions. For each one, everyone submits a Modifier anonymously. The Director chooses three to add to the shared AI prompt. The Crew needs a usable answer; the secret Injector hopes the prompt goes wrong. After the third commission, players vote on who they think sabotaged the work.

Playtest setup

What you'll need

  • 4–8 people; target 25–40 minutes.
  • One shared text-AI chat; no image generation.
  • One pen and small slips of paper.
  • Client Brief, Modifier, Role and Investigation cards from the alpha kit.
  • Run exactly three commissions. Keep all role assignments and author reveals according to the alpha rulebook.

Rules are provisional and have not been validated by observed human playtests. The kit is available by request rather than as an unrestricted public download.

Keep score here.

Record each commission's three criteria and forbidden word here. The tracker calculates the result, but the players still decide ambiguous cases. Nothing is sent to a server.

Scoring / v0.3 alpha

0 of 3 commissions passing

Enter all three commission outcomes.

Each commission passes if at least two of three required criteria pass and the forbidden word is absent from the scored answer.

Crew victory: all three commissions pass, OR exactly two pass and the group correctly identifies the Injector as the unique top-voted suspect. The Injector wins otherwise. An unresolved accusation is not a win.
