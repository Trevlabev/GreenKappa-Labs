Pick your kind
of chaos.

Each prototype has a different center of gravity: persuasive deception, collaborative absurdity, or ruthless control over what happens next. All three are original GreenKappa Labs concepts.

01 / Social bluffing54-card prototype

The Trial of
a Dead God

The deity is dead. Everyone has a motive. The verdict is yours to corrupt.

Put a murdered god on trial in a courtroom where nobody agrees on the facts. Assemble contradictory evidence, challenge testimony, and persuade the other players to believe your version of the impossible.

Each player has a private stake in the verdict. The best argument might win. The truth might not.

Debate & deceptionHidden agendasContradictory evidence

Design focus: an accessible, highly replayable argument game with enough structure to make every accusation matter.

02 / AI-assisted party comedy72-card prototype

Unsupervised.

Give an AI one job. Then make it everyone's problem.

A party game where players submit ridiculous ideas, a rotating operator brings them to a shared AI chat, and the results become an increasingly unhinged fictional canon.

The cards set the comedy in motion, but the table decides what survives. Each round adds another layer of improvisation, callbacks, and perfectly avoidable consequences.

One shared AI chatCreative sabotageEvolving canon

Design focus: keep the people, not the AI, at the center of the joke. AI access is part of the current prototype experience.

03 / Fate & social strategy54-card prototype

Prophecy
Is a Weapon

The future is written. The ink is negotiable.

Everyone has glimpsed a different future, and each vision contradicts the others. Trade omens, manipulate interpretations, and steer the table toward the version of destiny that serves you best.

Sometimes you win by fulfilling a prophecy. Sometimes the cleverer move is persuading everyone that it meant something else.

Secret objectivesNegotiationFate manipulation

Design focus: make prediction itself a playable resource, turning information and persuasion into tactical choices.
