Interpretation limits

The 16 fixed fixtures prioritize full card coverage; the four answers in a call are jointly generated, so they are not independent isolated agents. Three observations per appearance remain small. The model scores rules literally, but its judgment is not a human vote and its 1–5 comedy score is not an audience reaction.

Scenario-generation text is locked to the baseline during this experiment. Revised Scenario wording, game pacing, laughter, player choice and balance require separate human or factorial tests. No deck is automatically promoted to approved canon by this page.
